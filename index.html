OrbitX Privacy Policy

Applies to: OrbitX for Android (package com.orbitx.browser), version 1.0.0 and later.
Last updated: October 11, 2026
Developer: Uğur Berk Bozoğlu · Contact: berkbozoglu.dev@gmail.com

In short

OrbitX is a web browser. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no OrbitX server. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data. Your browsing data, bookmarks, settings and saved passwords stay on your phone.

What stays on your device

  • Browsing data: history, open tabs, cookies and site storage, downloads list, bookmarks, site permission choices and settings. You can delete browsing data in Settings → Privacy. Private tabs are not written to disk.
  • Saved passwords (optional): if you choose to save a password, it is encrypted with an AES-256-GCM key kept in the Android Keystore and stored in the app's private storage. Viewing, copying, editing or exporting a password requires your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Passwords are never sent anywhere. Private tabs never offer to save or fill passwords. The password screens block screenshots.
  • Backups you create: an export file is encrypted with a passphrase you choose (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, AES-256-GCM). It is created only when you ask, and you decide where it goes. OrbitX does not upload it, and the app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Network requests OrbitX makes

Other than loading the web pages you open, OrbitX connects to the servers below. None of these requests include your browsing history, passwords or an identifier; the servers can see your IP address and standard request headers, as with any web request.

Websites you visit can use cookies and other technologies; OrbitX blocks many trackers and third-party cookies by default and sends the Global Privacy Control signal. Websites and search engines have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

OrbitX asks Android for location, camera or microphone access only when a website requests it and you allow it in OrbitX’s own prompt first. The data goes to that website, as you directed, not to us. You can review or reset these choices in Settings → Site permissions.

Children

OrbitX is a general-purpose browser and is not directed to children under 13.

Open-source components

The list of open-source software and filter lists, with their licenses and links to their sources, is in Settings → About → Credits.

Your choices and deleting data

There is no account to delete. Everything OrbitX stores is on your phone: clear it in Settings → Privacy, delete saved passwords one by one in Settings → Passwords, or uninstall the app to remove all of it. Backup files you exported are ordinary files that you manage yourself.
